Help with STBPlay for Roku.

Contact

Email krsptrbeez@gmail.com and mention STBPlay for Roku, the version (shown in Settings > About STBPlay) and your Roku model. Do not send passwords or full playlist addresses that contain logins.

Common questions

There are no channels or movies

STBPlay includes no content. Open Sources and add your own M3U playlist, Xtream Codes account or Stalker portal.

My source will not load

Check the address, username and password, or the MAC address registered with your provider. A message saying the portal did not accept the session (Unauthorized) means the provider rejected the device: check that the MAC address is active on your account. Choose Try again on the Live TV, Videos (VOD) or Series page.

A stream will not play

Quality and availability depend on your provider, your network and your Roku model. Streams must be in a format Roku can play, such as HLS, MP4 or MKV. Try Playback quality in Settings, or ask your provider for an HLS output.

The program guide is empty

The guide comes from your provider. If your provider supplies none, or supplies it as a compressed file, the page shows "No guide information". Live TV still plays.

The Live TV preview is slow or off

The preview is a small extra player. You can turn it off or on in Settings > Live TV preview; it is off by default on older Roku devices. Press OK on a channel to watch full screen.

Purchases and the trial

Payments are handled by Roku. Use Settings > Restore purchases to check your Roku account, or contact Roku support for billing questions.

Delete my data

Settings > Delete my data removes sources, logins, favorites, history, the PIN and settings from your Roku. Removing the channel also removes its stored data.
