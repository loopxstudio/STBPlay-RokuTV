Bring your own playlists and provider accounts to STBPlay, a media player designed for Android TV and Google TV.

Browse live channels, movies and series with a remote-friendly interface. STBPlay supports Stalker portals, Xtream Codes-compatible accounts, and M3U/M3U8 playlist URLs. Available content and features depend on your provider.

WATCH AND BROWSE
• Browse channels and titles by folder or category.
• Search your catalog and keep favorites for easier access.
• Resume supported on-demand videos and find recently watched titles.
• Browse supported Stalker series through seasons, episodes and available files.

MANAGE YOUR SOURCES
• Save up to four named profiles.
• Refresh or reconnect, edit, and delete saved profiles.
• Use collapsible source forms to keep setup simple.

PLAYBACK CONTROLS
• Navigate playback controls with your TV remote.
• Adjust player volume and choose available audio/subtitle tracks.
• Import supported subtitle files using Android's document picker when available on your device.
• Enable an optional PIN to lock the app when opening it.

YOUR DATA
STBPlay has no publisher-operated account, advertising SDK or analytics service. Profiles, favorites and recent viewing are stored on your device. Requests needed for authentication, catalog browsing and playback go to the provider and content servers you select. You can clear viewing history or all local app data in Settings. See the privacy policy for details.

IMPORTANT
STBPlay does not include channels, movies, TV subscriptions or a preloaded playlist. You must supply a compatible source that you are authorized to access. The app is not affiliated with content providers. Source availability, picture/sound quality, supported formats, DRM restrictions and playback performance depend on your provider, network and device. The optional PIN is an app-opening lock, not a content-rating filter.

Designed for Android TV and Google TV; a TV remote is required.
