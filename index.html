STBPlay for Roku

Play your own M3U, Xtream Codes and Stalker sources on your TV.

A player for your own sources

STBPlay is a media player for Roku, built for the TV remote. You bring your own playlists and provider accounts: M3U/M3U8 playlist addresses, Xtream Codes-compatible accounts and Stalker (Ministra) portals. What you can watch depends on your provider.

STBPlay includes no channels, movies, subscriptions or playlists. Use only sources you are authorized to access. STBPlay is not affiliated with any content provider.

Features

  • Live TV with folders, numbered channels, search, a preview window and a simple now/next program guide when your provider supplies one
  • Videos (VOD) and series by folder, with search, favorites and resume
  • Next episode plays automatically; Up and Down change live channels
  • Up to four saved sources
  • Automatic, Low-power or High-quality playback
  • Optional PIN lock when the channel opens
  • English and French (Canada)

Free trial and pass

Try STBPlay free for 30 days. After the trial, playback needs an STBPlay pass bought through Roku Pay: 1 month or 1 year, one payment, no subscription and no automatic renewal. Roku shows prices in your currency and handles the payment; STBPlay never sees payment details.

Your data

No STBPlay account, ads or analytics. Sources, favorites and history stay on your Roku and can be deleted in Settings. Read the privacy policy and the terms of use.
